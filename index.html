About Dr. Amgad

Dr. Amgad ElKassas is a consultant obstetrician and gynecologist who cares for women through pregnancy, childbirth, and every stage of women's health. He sees patients at three clinics in Cairo.

  • Consultant
  • Obstetrics

How we can help

  • Pregnancy follow-up

    Regular check-ups and guidance from the first weeks to the due date.

  • Delivery and postnatal care

    Support during delivery and recovery afterwards.

  • Gynecology consultations

    Diagnosis and treatment for women's health concerns.

  • Family planning

    Advice on choosing the method that fits you.

  • Fertility consultations

    Assessment and a clear plan if you are trying to conceive.
